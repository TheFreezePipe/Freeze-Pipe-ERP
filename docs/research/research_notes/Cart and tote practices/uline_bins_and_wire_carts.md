Prices are list prices, US$, per bin / per cart unless stated. "1 ctn" = price when buying one carton; "3+ ctns" = price at three or more cartons. Prices shown without a date are not in this file. Notation: Uline lists shelf bins as **W x L x H** (width across the opening x length = front-to-back depth x height). Its category headings call the L dimension "deep" (12", 18", 24" DEEP). So Uline's "4 x 12 x 6" is the same bin the request calls "12-deep, 4-wide, 6-high".

## A. Uline plastic shelf bins (and the other bin lines that could be used on a cart)

### Takeaway
Uline's "Plastic Shelf Bins" come in 12", 18" and 24" deep ranges, widths 2 3/4 / 4 / 7 / 8 1/2 / 11", heights 4 / 6 / 8" (8" high only in 12" deep, 4 and 7" wide). Uline has NO 18 x 16 x 8, 24 x 16 x 8 or 18 x 11 x 10 shelf bin. The closest larger open-front-style bins are the Stackable Bins (18 x 11 x 10 = S-12421, 18 x 16 1/2 x 11 = S-12422) and the Divider Boxes (22 1/2 x 17 1/2 x 8 = S-16978), which is the bin Uline itself ships on its own wire picking cart.

### Inferences
- A 7 x 18 x 6 (S-15645, 5.30 to 5.45 per bin) or 8 1/2 x 18 x 6 (S-15646) shelf bin is the cheapest sensible "tote slot" at 18" depth; the 11 x 18 x 6 (S-15647, $12.05 to $12.50) is the widest 18-deep option that stays under 6" high. For a bigger "order tote" the Divider Box S-16978 (20 x 15 x 8 inside) is the only bin Uline proves works on a wire cart, because it ships that way (see B).
- Shelf bins sit on wire shelving with an open (cut-down) front, so the "open-front" requirement is met by the Plastic Shelf Bins line, but Uline's own text never says "open front"; my reading rests on the 2 7/8" front inside height on a 6"-high bin.
- Because the bins are NOT stackable and have a rear notch designed for Shelf Bin Organizers, on a flat wire shelf they rely on friction and the shelf's wire pattern; Uline sells no clip or lock for them (see D).
- Posts take about 2 3/4" off a wire shelf's usable width/depth (Uline spec for its picking cart; assumed similar for its other 1" post chrome shelving, **inference**). So 18"-deep bins need a 24"-deep shelf (usable about 21 1/4"), and 24"-deep bins (outside actual 23 5/8") will not fit on a 24"-deep shelf between posts. 12"-deep bins are the maximum that fits an 18"-deep shelf (usable about 15 1/4").

### Gaps
- Exact 18 x 16 x 8, 24 x 16 x 8 sizes requested: Uline does not list them in the shelf-bin line, stackable-bin line, or divider-box line (checked 2026-10-20). Whether another vendor (Akro-Mils) sells them was out of scope.
- Colors per individual size not verified; gray availability for shelf bins not confirmed.
- Divider pack quantity for every divider row not confirmed.
- Clear Shelf Bins and Giant Stackable Bins specs not opened.

## B. Uline wire carts, mobile wire shelving, shelf liners

### Takeaway
Uline's chrome heavy-duty wire carts come only in 2- and 3-shelf versions and are only about 41" tall overall (3-shelf 48x24 = H-1521, $250 / $240). Four-shelf units are Uline's "Chrome Mobile Shelving" at 60, 69, or 78" tall (48 x 24 x 60 = H-11891, $280 / $270). All use 5" locking swivel casters, adjustable shelves, and 1,000 lb listed capacity. Solid shelf liners (1/16" clear acrylic or black polypropylene) cost $29 to $37 per liner at 48x24 / 60x24 and are sold in multiples of 4. Uline also sells a ready-made wire picking cart (4 slanted shelves + 8 bins, H-11753, $560 / $545) which is the benchmark for the DIY.
